About Tankoyomi

Tankoyomi is the language-aware chapter router used by Colosseum Chapter Mode. Its provider inventory comes from the bundled manifest, while your language, enablement, and priority choices are saved locally.

Auto-pick tries the highest enabled provider first, then continues down the selected language’s ladder. Unsupported languages never fall through to another language.

All illustrative language/provider values are local examples, not a live inventory. The title, copy, flags, glass panels, ranking badges, letter tiles, inline default-language dropdown, toggles, arrows and tab layout are reconstructed from TankoyomiConfigurationPage.qml and TankoyomiFlag.qml. For phone and landscape, panels stack and provider actions can wrap; no alternative sheet is introduced. Insets and keyboard area are hypothetical and adjustable outside the mock.
