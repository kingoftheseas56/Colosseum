## Page-specific fitting decisions

**Theatre (`theatre-catalogue.html`).** Retains Movies, Shows and Anime tabs and their shelf inventory. The original 148px gallery art and 35px title reserve are used where available. On portrait phones with expanded index, poster width reduces to 128px and the content/index gap to 16px. On 354px wide, the approved fixed 240px rail plus its 28px gap would leave only approximately 50px inside the 318px board; a proportional in-place rail (`clamp(144px,48%,196px)`) is the smallest layout-only adjustment that keeps an actual poster visible beside the index. The expanded rail is **never** an overlay or replacement sheet. Portrait labels wrap; landscape/full-width rail remains the 240px source dimension. Theatre row customization is simulated entirely in page memory.

**Manga (`manga-catalogue.html`).** Retains the loaded Popular Manga ranked strip, Your Collection and all seven delivered shelf definitions. Long label “Completed in Five Volumes or Fewer” wraps in the expanded index. Gallery-versus-classic styling distinction remains: Tankoban classic titles and in-poster rank captions were not silently replaced with Theatre gallery styling. With portrait expanded index, classic poster width fits at 120px; unchanged 132px elsewhere.

**Comics (`comics-catalogue.html`).** Includes the specific comics shelf ordering; every delivered Comics shelf uses the original ranked `TrendingTop10` strip with in-poster captions and ghost numerals. The seven curated catalogues, Most Stocked and publisher shelves retain their Explore doors; decade, Deep Shelves and Fan-Made Shelf do **not** invent an Explore/See all link. “Explore Comics” has no clickable See all header. The 800×1280 tablet remains tablet reach (not phone based solely on portrait orientation), keeps 240px expanded catalogue index and has the approved 72px bottom Colosseum world tabs. At 1280×800 Android tablet and 900×412 wide phone, the *separate* 92px world navigation is the current approved shell behavior. In 792×354 short landscape, bottom world navigation remains.

**All pages.** Horizontal poster rows scroll; long shelves and indices independently scroll vertically/horizontally as applicable. Genre mosaics reflow into 2 columns on narrow portrait phones and 3 on narrower landscape/tablet surfaces. Viewport W/H, safe-area top/bottom and font scaling 0.8–1.5 are editable **outside** the Colosseum frame. Source colors, fonts, labels and available actions take precedence over generic mobile patterns.

## Mock interaction boundary and behavior proposals

No behavior replacement is proposed or implemented. The original in-place rail expansion, header controls, section navigation and customizable row actions are demonstrated locally. A tab can switch Theatre Movies/Shows/Anime and a Tankoban Manga/Comics tab can open the corresponding local sibling HTML. The See all, Explore, tile/details, system buttons and other route doors report which existing destination they represent in the **external review status**, without faking a real provider/catalogue, opening a new Colosseum sheet or calling an app/backend. No account or provider action occurs. There is no Rows button and no “On this page” sheet.

## Art and representative-data disclosure

The production catalogue's covers are usually live URLs and are **not** bundled in this frozen reference. All images in this study are actual local project files, not blank or stock posters:

- Theatre: `reference/desktop/assets/wallpaper/sagas/` images for **Dune**, **Jurassic Park**, **Harry Potter**, **The Lord of the Rings**, **Sherlock Holmes**, etc. Catalogue title examples are from `Catalog.js` or the approved rows; a Dune saga image is **not** asserted to be the Dune: Part Two theatrical poster, and many per-item title/art pairs are deliberately unmatched illustrative stand-ins.
- Manga: `reference/desktop/assets/universes/one-piece/.../banners/` One Piece story-arc illustrations (East Blue, Arabasta, Enies Lobby, Wano, Egghead, etc.) used as stand-ins for the `Catalog.js` real manga name examples. They are **not** claimed to be actual Berserk, Vinland Saga, Jujutsu Kaisen or other manga covers.
- Comics: `reference/desktop/assets/universes/dcau/portals/`, `dcau/environments/`, `reference/desktop/assets/extensions/house/`, and `universes/star-wars/` imagery; these are **not** the original Invincible/Saga/Sandman covers. The title examples come from `Catalog.js`, while artwork is local product illustration.
- The Featured headings/blurbs are sourced from `Catalog.js`. Seeded continuation and collection progress is for layout only, not user data. Sample hover rating is illustrative and not derived from a live catalogue.

Each page explicitly displays this qualification above its frame. Capture mode suppresses only the **external** review controls/annotation, not any Colosseum UI.
