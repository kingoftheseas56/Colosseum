EPIC FANTASY  ·  BRANDON SANDERSON  ·  2010

The Way of Kings: Book One of the Stormlight Archive

“Life before death. Strength before weakness. Journey before destination.”

On Roshar, highstorms shape the lives of kingdoms and the people who struggle to survive them. Kaladin, once a surgeon’s apprentice and a soldier, finds himself enslaved on the Shattered Plains, where bridge crews are sent into battles few return from. He must find a reason to protect others while everything he believes about honor is being tested. In a distant city, Shallan Davar seeks knowledge from the scholar Jasnah Kholin, concealing a desperate plan of her own. The visions of Dalinar Kholin, a highprince and veteran of the wars, point toward a danger far greater than any struggle over territory. Their stories follow separate paths through a world of ancient oaths, unusual powers, and the consequences of long-forgotten decisions. The questions that bind them together grow larger as each tries to decide what kind of person to become when duty, survival, and trust collide.
