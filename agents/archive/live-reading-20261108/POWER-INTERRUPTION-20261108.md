## 1. Night of Nov 8: critical thermal hibernation, not idle sleep
- **2026-11-08 01:06:42 IST:** Windows System, Microsoft-Windows-Kernel-Power, event **88** (two records for one episode): "The system was hibernated due to a critical thermal event", ACPI thermal zone `\\_TZ.CPUZ`, `_HOT = 373K` (about 100°C). At **01:06:44**, Kernel-Power **42**: "Sleep Reason: Thermal Zone". Power-Troubleshooter **1** reports the low-power interval 01:06 to wake at **14:35:47 IST**. Windows boot time was **Nov 7 23:53**, which confirms no new Windows boot during this interval.
- Same ACPI CPU zone and threshold recurred **Oct 30 17:46:03 IST** and **Nov 5 18:58:36 IST** (each event 88 twice and event 42 once): **three distinct thermal episodes** in ten days, not six separate episodes.
- The trip value is firmware's HOT threshold, **not** an independently measured CPU-core peak. A hardware cooling limitation, obstruction/dust, fan fault, dried thermal paste, unusual sustained load, or a firmware/sensor misreport remains to be distinguished; a thermal event itself is conclusively logged.
- `powercfg /a`: S3 standby and hibernate available; S0 Modern Standby unsupported. `powercfg /query SCHEME_CURRENT SUB_SLEEP`: AC and DC `Sleep after = 0` (Never), `Hibernate after = 0`. This was **not an idle timer**. AC lid-close behavior still maps to Sleep but does not explain the specific logged Thermal Zone reason.
- Plugged-in power plan is **High performance** with AC **minimum processor state 100%, maximum 100%, boost mode 2**, active cooling. That is an aggressive performance *policy*, not literal constant 100% CPU utilization, and a **plausible contributor** under heavy compiling, not proof of a cooling-part defect. Battery snapshot ~96%; charging/full battery cannot avert thermal protection.
- ACPI WMI currently shows CPUZ ~33°C / EXTZ ~48°C / LOCZ ~52°C; these firmware thermal-zone snapshots are **not a trustworthy CPU-core peak history**, and some other zones report implausible ~0°C. No safe sustained load/thermal hardware proof was attempted.
