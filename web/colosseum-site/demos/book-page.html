Theo of Golden

One spring morning, a stranger named Theo arrives in the small Southern town of Golden. At the local coffeehouse, he discovers pencil portraits of the town's residents hanging on the walls. He begins buying each drawing, then giving it to the person pictured, asking only to hear their story. As neighbours share old memories and unspoken regrets, simple gestures bloom into friendships, discoveries and unexpected change. Theo offers little explanation for his journey, but his curiosity draws people together in ways they could not have imagined. A novel about generosity, connection and the quiet power of being truly seen, Theo of Golden finds extraordinary meaning in ordinary lives.
