## Staged on purpose
- The chosen book title/author/genre/year, cover subject, synopsis **wording**, edition/audiobook formats, file sizes, languages, counts and availability, and idle unsaved Library state are representative demo data, not source-truth values. This does **not** excuse the separate 400-character API cap or missing structural TORRENTS surface documented above.
- The chosen status **“Read will choose a tracked edition”**, default **“Read”** and **“+ Library”** correspond to legitimate app states, but the app can also show disabled Read, **Continue**, **Read when ready**, Ready/progress/error status text, and **✓ In Library** depending on actual local state. These data/state values are not independently marked wrong solely because this noninteractive demo freezes one state.
- The 16,000 ms showcase loop and its frozen sample points at **t=0, 4,200, 7,000, 10,000, 12,200 ms** are presentation staging. Its exact automatic scroll/focus chronology is not claimed as an interaction-parity requirement; visual errors introduced by the false layout and gold-on-gold highlight are listed separately.
