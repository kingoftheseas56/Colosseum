# Extensions, wave 3: screenshot-led HTML recreation

Built `demos/extensions.html` as a standalone, fixed 1280 × 720 HTML stage with inline CSS and JavaScript, no framework, no remote requests, no external scripts, and no dependency on the live app. The one vertical scroller contains the Extensions House with Search, eight real-logo House tiles, and eight Store rails in their QML order: Essentials, Most popular, Play sources, Something to watch, Anime, World cinema, Subtitles, and Better titles. Every visible Store card uses a bundled local logo; no fake initial-letter logos or screenshot crops are used. The backdrop is the local Jolly Roger image under the dark veil, not the normal cold-ripple home wallpaper.

I opened and inspected `app-shots/13-extensions.png` and `app-shots/13b-extensions-store.png` before authoring. I read `demos/feria.html` for the stage contract and read the existing `current-ui-mock/demos/extensions.html`, its CSS, JavaScript and logo matcher as starting material. I inspected read-only `git show master:qml/...` source for ExtensionsStorePage, ExtensionsHouseHero, ExtensionsStoreCard, ExtensionsStoreRail, ExtensionsStoreApi, WorldPage, TopBar, Taskbar, Theme, Glass, and RowIndexSidebar. Nothing in the Colosseum repository or current-ui-mock was edited.

The House is 1172 × 330 CSS px at x=54, with the scroll viewport starting at y=96 and the board starting 12 px below that. Search sits 26 px from the House right edge and 20 px from its top. The glass installed tray is 94% of the House width, 174 px high, 22 px above its bottom, and holds eight 122 × 140 px slots. Each logo tile is 82 × 82 px with a 58% contained local logo, 19 px rounding, an individual name pill, and a green 28 px source tick. The screenshot's installed House sources are Nyaa, Tankoyomi, GetComics, Tankorent, LibGen, and AudioBookBay. Tributes and Grand Database intentionally have no green tick.

The screenshot has only the back arrow, the “Extensions” title and the subtitle. Accordingly there are no world pills, clock, system icons, or Vinyl pill in this page, although the general world-page contract includes them on other worlds. The pinned bottom-left two-button dock follows the Taskbar's 130 × 64 px collapsed capsule. Extensions has no visible RowIndexSidebar or in-page tab bar in the cited QML or screenshots, so neither was invented. The page's index and tab URL parameters are not required to display a sidebar or tabs that Extensions does not own.

Each Store rail has 26 px Fraunces title, 14 px subtitle, 14 px header-to-cards gap and a horizontal strip of 365 × 299 px cards with 22 px gaps. Each card contains a 365 × 220 px art panel, 112 × 112 px centred dark logo stage, local real logo, an approximately blurred/saturated logo wash, two lines of description, and a gold installed badge when applicable. Hover raises the card and sharpens its border; House tiles raise too. The single vertical page is wheel-scrollable with a narrow native scrollbar; store strips are horizontally scrollable. Search, See all, House source ticks, Escape-to-close and card information are lightweight local interactions rather than simulated account or installation calls.

The running screenshots show the DCAU graphic in the House. `ExtensionsHouseHero.qml` instead initializes a Star Wars banner and rotates across Star Wars, DCAU and Cosmere every 7.5 seconds. For screenshot fidelity and the explicit no-timeline rule, this recreation starts on the static local `dcau.jpg`; `?banner=starwars` or `?banner=cosmere` allows alternate static views.

The running Store screenshot shows MediaFusion, AIOStreams and ThePirateBay+ together just before the Play sources rail. The earlier mock's Most popular fixture instead starts with Cinemeta and does not contain ThePirateBay+. I used the screenshot order and the bundled real Pirate Bay logo for the staged first three cards; the full live ranking cannot be reproduced offline because `ExtensionsStoreApi.js` fetches and sorts the current stremio-addons.net catalogue. The selected later row membership, descriptions not visible in the two captures, and any displayed installed states outside clearly identifiable screenshot badges are staged fixture choices, not claims about today's live catalogue or complete account installation state.

Other deliberate values chosen rather than measured are the `?y=837` initial scroll offset for the middle capture, the additional store card background gradient colours, the per-logo CSS wash opacities and blur strength, and the simplified local search/details dialogs. The main section offsets, card dimensions, page margins, palette tokens, font sizes and dock dimensions came from QML and screenshots. The fixed stage is 1280 × 720 as the website contract demands, whereas the 1920 × 1008 captures represent approximately 1280 × 672 CSS px at 150% scaling. Therefore the final 720 px captures show 48 more CSS pixels vertically and the bottom-anchored dock sits 48 pixels lower relative to the source screenshot's top edge.

Exact visual differences that remain: QML renders real composited `MultiEffect` background blur, saturation and alpha through Qt, while local Chrome CSS blur and gradient washes cannot make precisely the same colour field. The live remote MediaFusion mark includes a white branded label; the bundled local transparent logo does not, so a small typographic wordmark was placed alongside that authentic logo within a white inset. The third visible Play sources card in the running screenshot has a purple mark, while the bundled Torrentio logo is green; the HTML keeps the actual bundled Torrentio artwork instead of inventing a purple logo. Store names/order after the first screenshot-confirmed cards may vary with the live API. The still image also cannot reproduce the app's native Qt keyboard collection focus/park or actual installation/configuration backend.

I rendered and visually inspected `reports/extensions-wave3-top.png` with the required Chrome headless 1280 × 720 command and `reports/extensions-wave3-scrolled.png` from `extensions.html?y=837`. Both files are real 1280 × 720 PNGs. A local file check found 28 bundled store-logo references and zero missing logo files. A separate headless DOM check rendered all eight Store sections and confirmed that `?tab=search` opens the dialog with its search field shown. No Colosseum application window was driven and no repository changes, commits or pushes were made.
