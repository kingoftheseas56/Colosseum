Two live production mutations were applied one at a time, built, observed failing, and restored with apply_patch:

1. Replacing the atomic token allocation with constant token `1` made K08-F2 fail its exact-once cancellation assertion. This proves collisions cannot silently preserve sibling ownership.
2. Replacing empty-input normalization with an early return made K08-F2 fail the exact fallback assertion.

After restoration, the focused case passed three consecutive executions and combined K08 passed 4/4.
