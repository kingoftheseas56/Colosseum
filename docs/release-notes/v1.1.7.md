# Colosseum 1.1.7

## Your Colosseum

Highlights and stats from what you read, watch, and listen to, using activity recorded by Colosseum.

## Stremio sync

Connect your Stremio account to sync your library, progress, and watched state.

## Trakt

If Trakt is linked to your Stremio account, Theatre sends playback history through that connection. Colosseum does not store your Trakt credentials.

## Also new

- Subtitle controls were redesigned.
- IMDb Continue Watching cards are back.
- Better text contrast on light wallpapers.

If you're on 1.1.6, update normally. If you're on 1.1.5 or earlier, install 1.1.7 manually once because the updater signing trust root changed in 1.1.6.

Download: `Colosseum-1.1.7-setup.exe`
