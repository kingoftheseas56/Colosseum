# Colosseum 1.1.7

Colosseum 1.1.7 is about three things: your own Colosseum, your Stremio account, and the services you track with.
Colosseum now keeps its own record of what you read, watch and listen to, brings your Stremio library and progress
with you, and sends what you watch to Trakt through your Stremio account. The Windows installer remains the stable
release artifact.

## Your Colosseum

A new page shows highlights and stats from what you read, watched and listened to. It is built only from what
Colosseum itself recorded on this profile, never from outside trackers.

## Stremio sync

Connect your Stremio account and Colosseum brings over your library, your progress and what you have already
watched, then keeps both sides moving together. Synced titles appear on Theatre's cards, watched state follows the
order you actually watched in, and long-running series are handled correctly. Account adoption keeps your Stremio
link, and interrupted syncs recover on their own.

## Trakt through your Stremio account

Colosseum is a Stremio client, so if your Stremio account is linked to Trakt, Colosseum uses that link. What you
play and pause in Colosseum reaches your Trakt history, and Stremio's Trakt add-on is installed for you. Link Trakt
once at web.stremio.com; Connections shows Trakt as "Via your Stremio account" with its linked status. No Trakt
password or token is stored by Colosseum.

## Also in this release

- Extensions is now a Colosseum world of its own, with add-on setup kept inside Colosseum.
- Subtitle controls are redesigned, and Continue Watching cards from the IMDb catalogue are back.
- World text reads better on light wallpapers.

Coming from 1.1.6, Colosseum updates itself as usual. Coming from 1.1.5 or earlier, install 1.1.6 or this
release manually once (see the 1.1.6 notes).

## Download
