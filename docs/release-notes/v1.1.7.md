# Colosseum 1.1.7

Colosseum 1.1.7 is the External Sync release. Your Stremio library, watch progress and history come into
Colosseum and keep moving with it, on a sync layer built so more services can plug in without another rewrite.
It also brings a new Extensions Store, tracker connections, your own history highlights and stats, and
redesigned subtitle controls. The Windows installer remains the stable release artifact.

## Stremio sync

Connect your Stremio account and Colosseum brings over your library, your progress and what you have already
watched, then keeps both sides moving together. Synced titles appear on Theatre's cards, watched state follows the
order you actually watched in, and long-running series are handled correctly. Account adoption keeps your Stremio
link, and interrupted syncs recover on their own.

## Tracker connections

The new Sync Center connects Trakt, Simkl, MyAnimeList and AniList to your Colosseum profile. Connections follow
your profile across sign-in and sign-out and stay separate from Stremio sync.

## The Extensions Store

Extensions is now a Colosseum world of its own. The House at the top holds Colosseum's own sources (Nyaa,
Tankoyomi, GetComics, Tankorent, LibGen, AudioBookBay) and the Universes. Below it sit the Essentials and rows from
the stremio-addons.net catalogue: Most popular, Play sources, Something to watch, Anime, World cinema, Subtitles
and Better titles, each with See all, plus search across every add-on. Add-ons that need setup are set up inside
Colosseum, never in an outside browser.

## Your Colosseum

A new history page shows highlights and stats from what you read, watched and listened to, recorded by Colosseum
on this profile only.

## Subtitles

Subtitle controls are redesigned, with font selection and a tidier appearance panel.

## Also in this release

- Continue Watching cards from the IMDb catalogue are back.
- The world text reads better on light wallpapers.
- Title pages no longer carry the world pills.

Coming from 1.1.6, Colosseum updates itself as usual. Coming from 1.1.5 or earlier, install 1.1.6 or this
release manually once (see the 1.1.6 notes).

## Download

`Colosseum-1.1.7-setup.exe` is the Windows installer for this release.
