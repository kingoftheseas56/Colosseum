# Colosseum 1.1.10

Vault now opens with its own background. Configurable Tankoyomi and Tankorent add-ons use online delivery while downloads and torrent handling stay on your device. MangaSin can be enabled as an optional Tankoyomi source, and playback retries preserve the current position.

## Vault

- Vault opens with its own background instead of leaving the previous world visible underneath.
- The title, search and window controls share an aligned header. Search is centered, with a separate row in narrow windows.

## Add-on delivery

- Tankoyomi and Tankorent use Workers for online add-on delivery while downloads and torrent handling stay on your device.
- Existing source settings are preserved.

## MangaSin

- Enable MangaSin in Tankoyomi to search and read directly from your device, with signed add-on updates.
- The source is optional and starts disabled.

## Playback

- The video player handles loading failures and preserves the playback position when retrying a failed stream.

## Linux accounts

- Accounts use the desktop Secret Service keyring.
- Locked or unavailable keyrings return an error without claiming credentials were deleted.

## Coming Soon

- Vinyl and Feria remain unavailable in this release. Vinyl stays blocked even when its development environment opt-in is set.

## Updates

- Windows update ordering recognizes 1.1.10 as newer than 1.1.9.
- Linux updates remain manual.
