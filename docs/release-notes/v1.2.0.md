# Colosseum 1.2.0

Personal Collections, more ways to browse manga, and improvements to reading continuity, Extensions and Downloads.

## Personal Collections

- Create private collections across supported media, add notes, and arrange titles in your own order.
- Collections stay local to your profile and work without an account. Hosted sharing and gifts remain planned for 1.3.0.

## Manga discovery and reading

- Browse Completed Series, Currently Publishing, short completed series, One-shots, and manga that began in the 1990s, 2000s or 2010s.
- Find existing Manhwa coverage through its dedicated category and publication filters. Manhwa series open in Chapter Mode.
- Remember each series' reading language and return through Continue to the correct translation, chapter and page.
- Improve Long Strip restoration and preserve numbered chapter progress when changing translations.

## Extensions

- Preview a manifest before installing from a URL.
- Manage installed source instances, configuration, enabled state and removal, with manifest artwork and clearer descriptions.

## Downloads and Vault

- Keep download-backed Vault shelves up to date, including show, season and episode grouping.
- Delete downloads in bulk, including download-owned Vault content, while preserving manually added Vault files.

## Windows integrations

- Optionally share local media over the local network through DLNA.
- AniList is an optional experimental integration for anime episodes and manga chapters. Live sign-in and synchronization were not release-qualified; this is not a Linux integration.

## Local media

- Retry failed local playback or reopen the session without restarting Colosseum.
- Open loose EPUB and comic Continue cards in their existing reader at the saved position.

## Availability and known limits

- Feria and Vinyl remain shaded and unavailable in these Windows and Linux installers, including through saved routes. Development environment opt-ins cannot enable them in shipping builds.
- Android and macOS are outside this release candidate.
- Some pages still lack minimize or maximize controls. Improvements in this release do not establish complete window-control coverage.
- Linux updates remain manual. Debian packages use XZ compression, matching the compression method used for 1.1.9.
