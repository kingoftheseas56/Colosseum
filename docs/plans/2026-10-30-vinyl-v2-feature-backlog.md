# Vinyl V2: deferred enrichment and capability backlog

Date: 30 October 2026. Companion: [foundation scope](2026-10-30-vinyl-foundation-scope.md).

Hemanth's current priority is a functioning end-to-end foundation. This record preserves the richer capabilities from both research audits so they do not expand that baseline or disappear. A basic capability can ship in the foundation while its advanced variants stay here. This is a product backlog, not an implementation plan, approved schedule, completeness claim or instruction to begin another lane.

**Placement correction:** Discover contains deliberate curated album guides. Explore is a catalogue browser with entity selection, explicit filters and sorting, using the other worlds' patterns. Neither receives automatic Top in your region, Trending or local chart shelves. Genre/region are facets; popularity is an ordering when supported. Regional/trending mechanisms retained in the research index are reference records, not authorization to add those rejected landing feeds. No hardcoded country is a substitute for the user's actual taste.

## Deferred feature families

1. **Richer mixes and radio:** multiple taste clusters; artist/genre/subgenre/decade/mood/activity/niche mixes; time/daypart adaptation; release/favorites/nostalgia families; multi-artist custom seeds; familiarity/variety controls; richer session steering; additional song/album radio seeds; advanced fatigue/diversity/repetition policy; paired-carousel presentation and delivered-but-unseen recommendation rotation.
2. **Editorial Discover expansion:** more scenes, eras, languages and regional guides; deliberately reviewed album rosters; richer context/influences; credited contributor tools; source coverage measurement; revisions and history; optional licensed publisher-list integrations. Preserve source rank versus listening order and a guide's canonical membership independently of playback availability.
3. **Catalogue identity and enrichment:** broader MusicBrainz/provider mappings, transliteration/aliases, editions/original release versus reissue dates, version provenance, region availability, language, label, explicitness and richer metadata coverage. Basic correct identity remains mandatory in the foundation.
4. **Music relationships:** verified related artists, collaborators, credited writers/producers/performers, samples/interpolations, covers/originals, group members/solo careers, influence routes and artist playlists. Do not conflate audience similarity with credited relationships.
5. **Explore depth:** the complete 57-dimension research matrix below; reliable compound filtering; explicit metadata coverage and unknown-value behavior. Mood/activity/genre editorial categories do not automatically classify every contained song.
6. **Advanced queue/session behavior:** recommendation injection into finite lists, recommended-next previews, deeper repeat control, save/restore session variants and acoustic sequencing. Continuous refill and truthful exhaustion belong to any foundational feature advertised as radio.
7. **Taste and privacy controls:** richer taste editing, explanations grounded in actual signals, scoped hide/block/snooze and undo, per-track/playlist training exclusions, recommendation recovery and profile/history controls. Preserve existing privacy and history-disable behavior throughout the foundation.
8. **Release and artist activity:** broader release feeds, upcoming releases, reminders/presave-like follow-up, optional artist/community updates and user-controlled notifications.
9. **Listening insights:** weekly/monthly/seasonal/yearly summaries, most-played and replay collections, local milestones and recap presentation. Global fan percentiles require independently supported population data.
10. **Library/offline depth:** richer saved-state search/filtering, subset-to-playlist workflows, saved stations versus finite snapshots, offline recent-music fallback and source-supported caching/download behavior. Foundation saves, playlists, local music and restart persistence are retained.
11. **Existing V2 media/context lane:** lyrics and synchronization, Genius annotations, music/live videos, alternate performances, short previews, lyrics search, credits and sourced song context. Retain the prior lane's provider contracts and qualification requirements; this backlog is not a replacement specification or authorization to modify its work.
12. **Social/shared listening candidates:** friend/family/shared-taste mixes, artist/listener bridges, public profiles and community recommendations, subject to privacy/account/moderation decisions. Core local music remains independent of these.
13. **Conversational candidates:** intent-to-playlist/station, suggested prompts, follow-up session steering, voice control, narrated DJ and language-localized presentation. Quality, ongoing cost and provider dependence need separate decisions.
14. **Acoustic analysis candidates:** BPM/key/mode, energy, danceability, valence, acousticness, speechiness, loudness/liveness and tempo/key-aware transitions. A competitor's internal metadata is not an available external dataset.

## Provider conditions excluded from the product promise

Free/Prime/Premium restrictions, skip limits, numbered All-Access allowances, artist-paid promotion and closed/private API access are research context. They are not features Vinyl commits to copying. Public metadata availability does not establish editorial-list, artwork or private recommendation access. The source comparisons below preserve those distinctions.
