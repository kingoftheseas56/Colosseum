# Home server logo provenance

These service marks are the corresponding SVG icon paths from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.37.0), pinned to tag `16.37.0`. The vector paths are unchanged; a root `fill` uses the color listed for each icon in Simple Icons' data: Jellyfin `#00A4DC`, Emby `#52B54B`, Plex `#EBAF00`.

| File | Source SVG | Brand source / guidelines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `jellyfin.svg` | [Simple Icons Jellyfin SVG](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.37.0/icons/jellyfin.svg) | [Jellyfin branding](https://jellyfin.org/docs/general/contributing/branding.html) |
| `emby.svg` | [Simple Icons Emby SVG](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.37.0/icons/emby.svg) | [Emby](https://emby.media); [Emby terms](https://emby.media/terms.html) |
| `plex.svg` | [Simple Icons Plex SVG](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.37.0/icons/plex.svg) | [Plex trademark guidelines](https://www.plex.tv/en-ca/about/privacy-legal/plex-trademarks-and-guidelines/) |

Simple Icons' repository license is CC0-1.0, but its [disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.37.0/DISCLAIMER.md) says CC0 does not necessarily apply to every included icon, leaves trademarks unaffected, and asks users to follow each brand's guidelines. The pinned Simple Icons entries for these three show no separate license label. Brand owners retain their trademark rights.

Jellyfin's branding rules permit naming Jellyfin to indicate interoperability, but do not explicitly authorize a third-party connector to display its logo; they forbid using the logo as that project's own logo. Keep the icon paired with a clear “Jellyfin” service label and out of Colosseum's app branding. Emby's official site identifies Emby as its service; no explicit third-party connector-logo permission was located. Plex expressly permits using its logo to show compatibility and requires visible trademark attribution; use “Plex, the Plex Play logo and Plex Media Server are trademarks of Plex and used under a license” or “* used under license from Plex” in the app near its Plex mark.
