Support
Colosseum.

For the art we carry with us.

Colosseum is a free, independent media app built around one idea: bringing different forms of art together in one place.

I'm developing Colosseum independently, and keeping it running comes with costs, from hosting accounts and backend services to maintaining and improving the app.

If you enjoy Colosseum and want to help keep it growing, you can contribute any amount you feel comfortable with. Even $1 helps.

There are no paid features, subscriptions, or exclusive perks. Supporting Colosseum is entirely voluntary, and the app is free and will stay that way.

Whether you contribute or simply use and enjoy Colosseum, thank you for being part of it.
