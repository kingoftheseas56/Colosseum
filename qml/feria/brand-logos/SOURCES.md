# Feria provider marks

These are recognizable provider marks used on Feria's service tiles and provider actions. The marks remain the property of their respective owners; their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

The SVGs for Netflix, HBO Max, Apple TV, Crunchyroll, YouTube, MUBI, Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Google Play Books, WEBTOON, and Kobo came from the [Simple Icons icon repository](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons). The provider color was applied to each monochrome SVG.

The PNGs for Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, Kindle, MANGA Plus, VIZ, DC Universe Infinite, Marvel Unlimited, and Apple Books came from each provider's site icon as served by [Google's favicon service](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons). They are stored locally so tiles do not depend on an icon request at runtime.

If a mark changes, replace only its file and keep the provider key in `PorticoCombinedGlyph.qml` stable.
